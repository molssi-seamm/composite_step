2026-11-11 Phase 1: recipes and the split energy level
=====================================================

Goal of v1
----------

The ``composite_step`` plug-in with the recipe library and SEAMM's composites as
model chemistries, ``SEAMM:CMP@<recipe>``. The thermochemistry step's quick and
default tiers become the first recipes and must reproduce today's numbers. The
design is ``NOTES_composite_methods.md`` (2026-10-29, Paul's decisions in its §10);
the presets it names (PBE0-D4) were replaced by r2SCAN-3c on 2026-11-08, and the
recipes follow the presets.
